Hi! I'm Hariel, a product designer based in Buenos Aires with 6+ years building B2B SaaS tools, most recently at Bitsight, a cybersecurity company.

Before design, I trained as an electronics technician in high school, which is where I took my first steps in programming. I later studied Graphic Design at Universidad de Buenos Aires. Digital products turned out to be a mix of both: the structured, systems-thinking of electronics, and the direct, human side of building software people actually use.

Somewhere in between, I spent a few years doing graphic design work, specializing in branding and editorial layouts. That background is still the reason I care so much about type, rhythm, and hierarchy in product work.

What I bring to a team: I move fluidly between shipping product screens and building the design system underneath them, so the two stay in sync instead of drifting apart. The work I care most about happens between wireframes and shipping: the alignment conversations with engineering, the edge cases nobody wants to spec, and the back-and-forth that gets a whole team, not just design, aligned on the same answer.
